Brendan Shrader

I am a Ph.D. student in the Quantitative Biosciences program at Georgia Tech, where I study mathematical questions arising in biology.

My interests include mathematical epidemiology, microbial population dynamics, differential equations, stochastic processes, and multiscale modeling. I am particularly interested in how mechanisms operating at cellular or within-host scales shape population-level behavior.

About

I completed a B.S. in Mathematics at the University of Central Florida in 2025, with a minor in Computer Science. My undergraduate research introduced me to mathematical epidemiology and led to an honors thesis on disease endemicity, partial immunity, and post-infection mortality.

I later worked on models of bacterial communication and population dynamics at Georgia Tech. My current interests sit at the intersection of these areas: combining deterministic and stochastic models to connect biological mechanisms across scales.
